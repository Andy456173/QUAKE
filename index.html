KNOW THE RISK

WHY EARTHQUAKES MATTER.

Myanmar is located in a seismically active region, and the Sagaing Fault passes through parts of the country. Earthquakes can damage buildings, bridges, roads, schools, and historic sites, sometimes with devastating consequences for local communities.

QUAKE is a student-developed ICT PBL website that makes historical earthquake information more accessible while encouraging people to prepare before a disaster takes place.

BE PREPARED

BE READY BEFORE IT STRIKES!

Small actions taken before an earthquake can help protect you, your family, and your school community. Explore three useful ways to prepare.

!
WHEN THE GROUND SHAKES

Remember these three things.

01Drop to your hands and knees.

02Cover your head and neck under sturdy furniture if you can.

03Hold on and stay inside until the shaking stops.

If you are outdoors, move away from buildings, trees, and power lines when it is safe to do so.

01 / PACK

EMERGENCY KIT.

Prepare water, non-perishable food, medicines, a flashlight, batteries, and essential emergency contacts.

Keep your supplies somewhere you can reach them easily. Include a first-aid kit, sturdy shoes, and copies of important documents. Check the kit regularly.

02 / PRACTICE

DROP, COVER, HOLD ON.

Know how to protect your head and neck and practice responding safely when the ground starts shaking.

Drop to your hands and knees, cover your head and neck under sturdy furniture if available, and hold on until the shaking stops. Do not run outside during shaking.

03 / PLAN

SAFETY PLAN.

Identify safe places, organize emergency contacts, and agree on a family meeting location for after an earthquake.

Secure heavy furniture, know where to shelter in each room, establish an emergency communication plan, and take part in school and household drills.

THE MANDALAY–SAGAING
EARTHQUAKE

On 28 March 2025, a magnitude 7.7 earthquake struck central Myanmar near Sagaing and Mandalay. Buildings, homes, public facilities, and vital infrastructure were heavily damaged. The disaster highlighted the importance of safer buildings and earthquake preparedness.

¹ UNICEF reported at least 3,745 deaths as of 13 June 2025. ² UNU estimated likely building damage across seven assessed cities, not Mandalay alone.
