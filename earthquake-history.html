THE BIG PICTURE / 01

A HISTORY WRITTEN
IN THE GROUND.

Myanmar has experienced repeated destructive earthquakes along major fault systems. Some historic events are known only from written accounts; later events have instrument measurements and more detailed disaster reports.

This timeline highlights selected major earthquakes, not every earthquake in Myanmar. Dates, magnitudes and casualty counts can differ between studies—especially before modern seismic instruments.

MORE THAN JUST A NUMBER.
