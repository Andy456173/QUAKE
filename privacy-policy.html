01 / WHAT WE HANDLE

Information we collect

QUAKE does not ask you to create an account or provide contact details. Some pages save your selected language in browser storage. If you agree to the Terms of Service, this browser also stores the terms version and acceptance time; no account is created.

When a web page is requested, the hosting provider and services used to deliver page resources may process basic technical information, such as an IP address, browser details, and the requested file, as part of normal internet delivery and security. QUAKE does not use this information to identify visitors.

02 / WHY IT IS USED

How information is used

The website is designed to provide earthquake education and preparedness information. It does not sell personal information, run user profiling, or use advertising trackers. Any technical request data handled by hosting or delivery providers is subject to those providers' own practices.

03 / SERVICES OUTSIDE QUAKE

External services and links

Some page resources are delivered by third parties, including Google Fonts, the Bootstrap CDN, and an image hosted through Wikimedia Commons. Following external links may also take you to other websites. When your browser requests those resources or sites, their providers may receive technical request information and apply their own privacy policies.

QUAKE does not control third-party services or websites. Review their privacy information directly if you want to understand how they handle data.

04 / YOUNG VISITORS

Information from children

QUAKE is an educational website and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the site does not provide forms or accounts, visitors should not send personal or sensitive information through any external service linked from this website.

05 / YOUR CHOICES

Your choices and browser settings

You can manage browser site data and withdraw your Terms of Service agreement below. Blocking third-party resources may affect fonts or photographs, but the educational content remains available.

06 / KEEPING THIS CURRENT

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the website's features or services change. The latest revision date is shown near the top of this page. Please check back when you want to review the current version.

07 / TERMS OF SERVICE

Terms of Service

VERSION 1.0

1. Educational purpose

QUAKE is a student-created educational website about earthquakes and disaster risk reduction. The information is intended for learning and general preparedness, not professional engineering, medical, legal or emergency advice.

2. Information and accuracy

We aim to cite reputable sources. Earthquake damage and casualty figures may be preliminary estimates and can change. Always consult current official advice and emergency authorities when making safety decisions.

3. Appropriate use

You may browse and share links for educational purposes. Do not misuse the site, attempt to interfere with its operation, or present its material as official emergency instructions.

4. Sources and external websites

Photographs, fonts, frameworks and external research links may belong to other providers and have their own licenses or policies. Respect those terms when reusing content.

5. Local agreement record

If you accept, this browser stores only the terms version and your acceptance time in localStorage. QUAKE does not send this agreement record to a server. You may withdraw acceptance below or clear your browser's site data.

6. Changes and availability

Content and these terms may be revised as the school project develops. A new terms version will ask you to agree again. The site is provided on an educational, as-available basis.
