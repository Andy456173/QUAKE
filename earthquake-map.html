ABOUT THE DATA

THE NUMBERS NEED CONTEXT.

Regional casualty figures are preliminary snapshots. Satellite-derived building damage is an estimate for seven cities only. They use different sources, geographic coverage, and reporting dates. Do not combine or compare these as if they were a complete census.
